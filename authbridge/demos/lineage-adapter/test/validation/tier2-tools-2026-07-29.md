## What these two runs establish

Every one of these traces is **one signal exchange among five or six**. Before
`6e8b0cca` the signal exchange was itself unparsed and would have been filtered
away with the plumbing, leaving the flow view **empty** for a tool called
directly. These are therefore the sharpest demonstration in the matrix both of
WS-1's noise filter and of why the parser chain had to be uniform.
